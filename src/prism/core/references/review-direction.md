# Review and Refine the Direction

## Purpose

This step exists to decide whether the explored direction is strong enough to move forward.

It is not only a vote on preference. It is a diagnosis step. When a direction is rejected or feels weak, the job is to understand what failed and step back to the right earlier point.

## The branch

Ask:

**Do we agree the direction is strong enough to structure and express?**

- **Yes:** move forward.
- **No:** diagnose the problem and step back only as far as needed.

Do not respond to disagreement by automatically making more options.

## What disagreement may mean

The disagreement may point to:

- the problem was framed incorrectly;
- the user situation is wrong or incomplete;
- the directions are too similar;
- the right direction exists but is explained poorly;
- the direction is right but the structure is weak;
- the artifact is weak, not the idea.

The job of this step is to identify which of these is happening.

## Diagnose before revising

Check:

1. Is the disagreement about the problem itself?
2. Is it about the user story or assumptions?
3. Is it about the type of solution being proposed?
4. Is it about trust, control, or risk?
5. Is it about hierarchy, sequence, or state handling rather than the direction?
6. Are the options too close to one another to create a real choice?

Also ask:

7. Is the disagreement really about the direction, or is the explanation too thin?
8. Is the idea sound but the artifact too weak to defend it?

## Step back to the right place

Use the smallest useful step-back:

- return to **Frame the real problem** when the challenge itself looks wrong;
- return to **Understand the user situation** when the narrative or assumptions are weak;
- return to **Competitive research** when the team lacks outside pattern confidence;
- return to **Explore possible ways to solve it** when the directions are too similar or incomplete;
- return to **Shape the experience** or **Define flows and states** when the direction is sound but the structure is weak;
- return to **Iterate the wireframe** when the idea is sound but the artifact is not communicating it.

## Output structure

```markdown

...

**What should be revised next**
...
```

## Quality check

- The step diagnosed disagreement before generating more options.
- The step-back decision is as small as possible.
- The output distinguishes direction problems from structure or artifact problems.
- The output distinguishes a weak direction from a weak explanation of the direction.
- The review creates a clear next move instead of vague dissatisfaction.
